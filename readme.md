# Finnish IT Job Market Analysis
## A Python-based analysis of technical skill demand in Finnish IT job advertisements.
### Research objective:
Understand which technical skills, job roles and locations appear in the collected Finnish IT job advertisements during a clearly defined period.
### Target audience:
IT job seekers, career changers, recruiters and people interested in the Finnish technology job market.
### Initial scope:

- Job advertisements published.
- Positions located in Finland.
- Software development, data, cloud and AI-related roles.
- Skills mentioned in the collected advertisements.
### Analysis question:

1. Which technical skills appear most frequently?
2. Which IT job titles are represented?
3. Where are the advertised positions located?
4. Which skills tend to appear together?
5. How do requirements differ by role or experience level?
6. What salary information is available?
